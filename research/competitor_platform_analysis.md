# Competitor Platform Analysis: Mental Health Services

This report details how major mental health platforms categorize their services, the conditions and specialties they cover, and their methods for matching users to providers.

## 1. BetterHelp
- **Categories/Specialties:** Anxiety, Depression, Bipolar Disorder, PTSD/Trauma, ADHD, Eating Disorders, Substance Use, Grief & Loss, Stress Management, Life Transitions, Self-Esteem, Relationship Difficulties, LGBTQ+ Affirming Care.
- **Provider Matching:** BetterHelp uses a comprehensive, research-backed onboarding questionnaire. It captures user goals, symptoms, and preferences (demographic, therapeutic style, logistics).
- **Matching Method:** Machine learning algorithm analyzes questionnaire inputs against the database of providers to match based on clinical expertise and compatibility. Users can switch therapists manually at any time.
- **Unique Features:** Emphasis on "therapeutic alliance" via demographic and style preferences. Offers video, phone, live chat, or messaging.

## 2. YourDost (India)
- **Categories/Specialties:** Career & Workplace (stress, performance), Relationships (family, love, social issues), Academic (pressure, time management, concentration), General Mental Health (anxiety, depression, personal growth).
- **Provider Matching:** Categorization-based routing. Users select a category of concern and follow platform prompts.
- **Matching Method:** Self-selection and platform routing based on chosen categories. Uses self-assessment tests to build emotional well-being awareness prior to matching.
- **Unique Features:** Anonymous chat, voice, or video appointments. Offers "weekly doses of self-awareness."

## 3. Talkspace
- **Categories/Specialties:** Broad spectrum of mental health conditions including anxiety and depression.
- **Provider Matching:** Uses a proprietary algorithm during the sign-up and intake phases. Factors include state of residence (for licensing), specific areas of concern, insurance/coverage, and user preferences (gender, language).
- **Matching Method:** Algorithmic match with human oversight/flexibility. Users receive a selection of potential providers and can also bypass the algorithm to browse the directory manually.
- **Unique Features:** Cross-references health plan benefits directly in the algorithm. Strictly filters by state licensure.

## 4. Amwell (formerly Amwell Psychiatry)
- **Categories/Specialties:** Anxiety Disorders (generalized, social, panic), Mood Disorders (major depressive disorder, postpartum depression, bipolar), Attention Disorders (ADD/ADHD), Trauma (PTSD), OCD, Adjustment Disorders, Insomnia.
- **Provider Matching:** Users browse and select providers (psychiatrists and therapists) based on availability, profiles, and specialties.
- **Matching Method:** Manual selection by the user.
- **Unique Features:** Focuses heavily on psychiatric diagnosis and medication management. Integrates with digital mental health programs like SilverCloud Health.

## 5. Cerebral
- **Categories/Specialties:** Anxiety, Depression, Insomnia, ADHD, Bipolar Disorder, Postpartum Depression, Trauma/PTSD. In some states: Substance use disorders and chronic weight management.
- **Provider Matching:** Users answer questions about their symptoms and are guided to suitable care plans (therapy, medication, or both).
- **Matching Method:** Questionnaire-driven guidance with manual selection of specific clinicians.
- **Unique Features:** Strong emphasis on medication management (prescribing and shipping medications) alongside talk therapy.

## 6. Calm / Headspace
- **Categories/Specialties:** 
  - *Headspace App:* Stress/Anxiety management, Sleep support, Emotional resilience, Clinical support (CBT for anxiety/depression, coaching, psychiatry for eligible members).
  - *Calm App:* Meditation, sleep quality, relaxation tools.
  - *(Note: headspace Australia focuses on youth mental health, physical/sexual health, work/study, alcohol/drugs).*
- **Provider Matching:** primarily self-guided digital therapeutics. For Headspace Health (clinical side), users are triaged to coaching or therapy based on assessment.
- **Matching Method:** Self-guided / AI-driven triage for clinical escalations.
- **Unique Features:** Focus on mindfulness, meditation, and preventative care before clinical intervention.

## 7. Woebot (Sunset June 2025)
- **Categories/Specialties:** General emotional challenges (stress, anxiety, depression symptoms), Postpartum depression, Adolescent support.
- **Provider Matching:** N/A (AI Chatbot).
- **Matching Method:** AI-driven conversational agent based on CBT, DBT, and IPT principles.
- **Unique Features:** Was "agnostic to diagnosis." Acted as a mental health ally for between-session support rather than a replacement for therapy.

## 8. Wysa
- **Categories/Specialties:** Anxiety, Worry, Depression-related symptoms, Stress, Life challenges, Loss and conflict, Sleep and relaxation.
- **Provider Matching:** Users interact with an AI chatbot first. Can upgrade to human coaching.
- **Matching Method:** Conversational AI uses NLP to detect emotional states and provide relevant CBT/DBT exercises. Human coaches are assigned based on user subscription.
- **Unique Features:** 24/7 AI chatbot acts as a non-judgmental space to vent. Uses AI to screen for distress and escalate to crisis helplines or human coaches if needed.

## 9. 7 Cups
- **Categories/Specialties:** Relationship problems, anxiety, depression, specific conditions like BPD. 
- **Provider Matching:** Users connect with volunteer "listeners" or licensed therapists.
- **Matching Method:** Users can instantly connect with the first available volunteer OR manually browse listeners filtering by topics/categories, language, and badges (e.g., Verified Listener).
- **Unique Features:** Group support chatrooms. Distinction between peer support (volunteers) and licensed professional therapy directory.

## 10. MindPeers (India)
- **Categories/Specialties:** Clinical (Anxiety, Mood Disorders, Trauma, Neurodivergence/ADHD), Relationship & Lifestyle (Couples/Family, Career, Financial stress), Mind-Body (Sleep, biomarkers), LGBTQ+ Affirming Therapy. Modalities include CBT, DBT, REBT, Arts/Animal-assisted therapy.
- **Provider Matching:** Users filter therapists based on language, expertise, and approach.
- **Matching Method:** Manual filtering by the user augmented by clinically validated pre-screener tests.
- **Unique Features:** Integrates neuroscience games, "MindShorts", and journaling tools alongside therapy.

## 11. Practo Mental Health (India)
- **Categories/Specialties:** Divided broadly into Psychiatry (Medical, Neuropsychiatry, Addiction) and Psychology (Talk therapy, Counseling, CBT).
- **Provider Matching:** Users search by concern (anxiety, sleep) or by specialty (Psychiatrist vs. Psychologist).
- **Matching Method:** Manual marketplace search with filters for experience, patient reviews, fees, and location (online vs. in-clinic).
- **Unique Features:** Aggregator model handling both online consultations and in-clinic physical appointments.

## 12. Manastha (India)
- **Categories/Specialties:** Common conditions (depression, phobias, sleep), Relationship & Family, Personal Growth (career, self-esteem, motivation), Specialized (Trauma, anger, parenting, spiritual, sexual, corporate wellness).
- **Provider Matching:** Users provide details about their concerns, and the platform assigns a suitable therapist.
- **Matching Method:** Needs-based matching (often involving an initial assessment or consultation) rather than pure self-selection.
- **Unique Features:** Standardized psychological assessments (personality, self-esteem) used to set therapeutic goals. Easy switching of therapists if not a good fit.
